# Use of AI tools

## Approved tools

Staff may use the organisation's enterprise AI assistant for drafting, summarising and searching
internal material. Other AI tools may be used only with public information.

## Restricted information

Restricted information must never be entered into an AI tool unless the tool runs on the
organisation's own infrastructure and has been approved for restricted use by the Digital Office.

## Human review

Any text produced with AI help must be reviewed by a person before it is sent outside the
organisation. The reviewer is responsible for its accuracy.
