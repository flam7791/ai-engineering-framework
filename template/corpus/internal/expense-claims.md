# Expense claims

## Deadline

Expense claims must be submitted within 60 days of the end of the trip. Claims submitted later
are paid only with the approval of the head of unit.

## Receipts

Original receipts are required for every expense above 25 euros. Photographs of receipts are
accepted if they are legible.

## Travel class

Economy class is the rule for flights under six hours. Business class may be approved for flights
of six hours or more.
